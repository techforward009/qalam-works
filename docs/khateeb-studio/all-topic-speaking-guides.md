## Sources and delivery

The guidance is Qalam Works editorial explanation, not a new scholarly quotation or historical transcript. Everyday examples are labelled hypothetical. Complex doctrinal sections distinguish the verse itself, Imami interpretation, analogies, and the supplied scholarly arguments. Practical ethical applications avoid replacing individual jurisprudential guidance with invented rulings.

Source verses are displayed in full using the existing Qur'anic corpus. Verse ranges are expanded and duplicate locations removed. Urdu and English translations retain their respective Najafi and Qara'i attribution. Further-reading leads are explicitly labelled as leads. Existing scholarly material and detailed research dossiers remain available below the guide.

Brief copying retains source verses, translations, questions, actions, and related scholarly summaries/references. Detailed copying additionally includes explanations, examples, transitions, and paraphrased scholarly material. Research dossiers have a separate copy button. The saved brief/detailed choice applies across topics; selected duration determines a suggested positive whole-minute allocation totalling 20, 30, or 45 minutes. These allocations guide expansion by the speaker and are not verbatim timed scripts.

Each new guide includes a section navigation list. Controls/navigation hide in print; selected guide content remains printable. Multi-sermon research/composition flows remain separate. Visual review also identified missing Qur'anic-sign fallback glyphs in English: the existing Muhammadi/Al Qalam preference now falls back to bundled Amiri/Naskh families through their actual local-font variables, preserving source characters.
